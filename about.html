Jiwon Kim
Works About

Jiwon Kim is a designer currently studying graphic design at ArtEZ University of the Arts in the Netherlands. Her work explores experimental graphic design based on research into materiality, structure, and visual perception, with a focus on books and printed matter.

Her work begins from an interest in the past and the future, reflecting on social and environmental issues to encourage deeper thinking about the world. By engaging viewers and inviting reflection, she aims to help people better understand and empathize with these social issues, and ultimately make positive change.
