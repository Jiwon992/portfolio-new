This live performance explores how a school, a place usually considered safe, might change if surveillance existed everywhere. A camera was installed on the ceiling of a hallway while mirror stickers were placed on the walls, floor, and ceiling.
The camera and mirrors transform the entire hallway into a surveillance space. The live-streamed footage broadcasts the reactions of people who notice the camera, and showing how surveillance can exist even in places we usually think of as safe.
